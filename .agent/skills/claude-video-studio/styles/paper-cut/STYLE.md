# Paper Cut

id: paper-cut
Best for: stories, travel, nature, gentle brand moments, loops.
Mood: a pop-up book. Layered cut paper with soft shadows and real depth.
Reference piece: styles/paper-cut/index.html (12 s, loops)
Gallery video: gallery/videos/paper-cut.mp4

## Palette (hex)
- cream: #F2E8D5
- sand: #E3CBA8
- sage: #9CB49A
- deep sage: #5E7F6A
- teal: #2F5D62
- ink: #2B2B2B
- accent: #D98C7A (dusk rose, one small thing: a sun, a flag, a boat)

## Type
- Mostly no words. If needed: EB Garamond 500 italic, 64 px or more.
- Words are paper too: each letter lands with its own small shadow.
- Font links (paste into <head>; all fonts are free):
  <link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=EB+Garamond:ital,wght@1,500&display=swap">

## Easing (numbers)
- sheet lands: back.out(1.2), 0.6 s, with a small 2% overshoot
- camera push: sine.inOut, slow, 4 to 8 s
- bob and sway: sine waves with a whole number of cycles (so it loops)

## Motion rules
- Every sheet has a hand-cut edge, a thin light rim on top and a soft shadow on the sheet behind.
- Near sheets move faster than far sheets (parallax).
- A sheet still in the air throws a wider, softer shadow that tightens as it lands.

## Signature moves
- The stack: sheets land one by one, far to near, each held 4% big with a wide shadow, then set down.
- The breath: in a hold, the sheets lift a little and settle again, one after another.
- The unstack: the same sheets lift off near to far until the bare cream page is back. That closes the loop.
- Leaves swing in on their stems from the corners, like a pop-up page opening.

## Grid
Layers, not columns: 5 to 7 depth layers from sky to foreground.

## Composition
- Text hangs off the left margin on bare cream. The shapes frame it: arcs on the right, waves below, leaves in the top corners.
- Big shapes bleed off the frame edges. Nothing floats small in the middle.

## With media
- Put a photo inside a paper window: cut a hand-cut hole in a cream sheet and lay it over the photo, with a shadow on the photo.
- Grade photos warm and soft, low contrast, so they sit with the paper colours.
- Paper leaves or waves can overlap the photo edge to tie it into the stack.

## Ban list
- flat vector shapes with no shadow
- perfect geometric edges
- bright saturated colours
- text on top of the scene
